EEG Quality Control Workflow

Where quality control fits in the EEG pipeline, and what to look for at each inspection point. Raw data are inspected before any processing is run; processed data are inspected again before they enter analysis.

Pipeline

  1. Data acquisition
    Record EEG data with careful setup, annotations, and metadata.
  2. Preprocessing
    Apply filtering, re-referencing, artifact correction, and epoching.
  3. Analysis
    Fit models, inspect outputs, and report findings.
